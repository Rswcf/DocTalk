# T1 — English-market competitor audit: free-tier terms, paywall triggers, one-off pricing

**Access date for every source in this document: 2026-09-22.** All prices, limits, and quotes below were read directly from each vendor's own public pages (or, where marked `secondary`, from third-party review/aggregator pages, which are not reported as fact). No account was created, no form was submitted, no payment or personal data was entered, and no cookie/consent banner was accepted beyond what was needed to read a public page (declined non-essential cookies where a preference panel was offered).

**DocTalk Free, for comparison (per research-lead brief, verified 2026-09-22):** 500 credits once at signup + 300/mo that never expire (Flash answer ≈ 5 credits, i.e. ≈60 Flash answers/mo; Pro answer ≈ 15 credits, capped separately at 20 Pro-model answers/mo); 3 documents; 3 open conversations per document; 50 MB / 750 pages per file; no export. Sign-in is required to upload — DocTalk has no anonymous-upload mode (only the separate 3-document demo, 5 msgs/IP/document/24h, is anonymous).

**Headline finding (detail in table + Evidence quotes):** of the 15 products audited, only **2** literally match "≈N free uses, then payment required" in the owner's sense of a hard, non-resetting lifetime cap — **ChatDOC** (10 files total, ever) and **PDF.ai** (1 PDF total, ever) — and neither N is "about three." **Scholarcy** is ambiguous (its own pricing page and its own FAQ state two different, seemingly-conflicting free limits). The other 12 use a rolling daily/monthly allowance that never permanently locks the product (perpetual freemium), a per-file size/page gate with no use-count at all, an unlimited-core-feature model, or — for Adobe — zero standing free AI use with a time-boxed trial as the only no-commitment path.

## Gaps

- **ChatPDF Plus price — not published anywhere on the vendor's site.** `chatpdf.com/pricing` and `/plus` both 404; the homepage/FAQ describe Plus only qualitatively ("unlimited document analysis"). Third-party sources disagree by a factor of 4 ($5–$19.99/mo) with no way to adjudicate among them from public pages — reported as `secondary` only, not as fact.
- **ChatDOC's dollar pricing is vendor-sourced but from a page dated 2023-11-22** (`chatdoc.com/blog/chatdoc-pro-plan/`); no current-dated pricing page exists (`/pricing` 404s). The figures match what third-party aggregators report today, but "still live and linked from the current FAQ" is the strongest verification available, not a freshly-dated vendor statement.
- **AskYourPDF's and Sharly's monthly-billed (non-annual) sticker prices are not shown** — both pricing pages display only the annual-billed monthly-equivalent figure plus a discount percentage, never the higher standalone monthly price.
- **Humata's Student tier could not be found on the current live pricing page**, despite an internal Feb-2026 project note recording one at $1.99/mo (.edu). Not confirmed removed — only confirmed absent from the page reached on 2026-09-22.
- **SciSpace's free-tier numeric allowance is not published anywhere** the vendor's own pages were reached — the pricing page shows no Free card at all (only four paid tiers), and the Chat-with-PDF landing page asserts a "free tier" with zero quantification. Several FAQ accordion questions about credits ("How can I check my available credits and credit consumption?", "Do unused monthly credits roll over?") were visible as headers but not expanded — answers not captured.
- **Payment-method logos were not visible without signup for 12 of the 15 products** (ChatPDF, AskYourPDF, Humata, PDF.ai, Sharly, Anara, SciSpace, Scholarcy, Consensus, Paperpal, NotebookLM, Adobe). For SciSpace and Paperpal specifically, a "What payment methods do you accept?" FAQ question was visible on the page but its answer was behind a collapsed accordion that was not expanded — a shallow gap (the text exists on the page) rather than a true signup-gate, but the answer itself was not captured either way.
- **Student/edu discounts: Paperpal's FAQ has a "Do you offer student discounts?" header that was not expanded** — recorded as not visible rather than assumed absent.
- **Currency/geo-pricing lock for Google NotebookLM and Adobe Acrobat:** both `one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/` and `www.adobe.com/acrobat/pricing/` rendered in German/EUR regardless of `?hl=en&gl=us`-style URL parameter overrides, in both the WebFetch tool and the interactive browser session used for this research. This is very likely a genuine geo-IP pricing lock on the vendor's side (both companies are known to localize Google One / Adobe pricing by detected region), not a page that was skipped — but it means the USD figures a US visitor would see were not independently confirmed in this session. EUR figures are reported as directly observed.
- **Scholarcy and Paperpal display EUR pricing too**, but neither vendor page gave a clear signal of whether this is geo-IP-based (like Google/Adobe) or simply the vendor's default currency for all visitors (Scholarcy is UK-based; Paperpal/Cactus Communications is Singapore-registered with a strong India/global academic user base) — recorded as observed, not resolved.
- **Two vendor pages for ChatDOC disagree on the OCR add-on price** ($0.1/page on the Pro-plan blog post vs $0.05/page on the FAQ page) — both quoted, neither discarded, no way to determine which is current from public pages alone.
- **Sharly's free tier shows both "5 docs/day" and "10 documents/month" on the same card** — arithmetically inconsistent if both are taken literally (5/day sustained would allow ~150/month). Reported as-is; not reconciled.
- **Scholarcy's own pricing page contradicts itself** between the free-tier card text ("Limit of 10 summaries," no period stated) and the adjacent FAQ ("up to 1 summary per day") — this is the one case in the set that could not be resolved to a confident yes/no on the owner's "≈N free uses" question, and is reported as ambiguous rather than forced into either bucket.
- **"Account before first use?" could not be tested empirically for any product** — per the task's safety rules, no signup/login/form-submission was performed. Every "email" or "none" classification in the table rests on (a) an explicit vendor sentence where one was found, or (b) the presence/absence of a working anonymous upload widget as observed on the page, never on actually attempting to use the product past that point. Docalysis's "Try Now" demo-file link and NotebookLM's "featured public notebooks" are two cases where some anonymous interaction may be possible beyond what was confirmed here — flagged, not claimed.

Attributing an In-App Purchase price to chatpdf.com under those conditions would risk reporting a false source, so none was used. For the other 14 products, web pricing was reachable directly and a mobile-store fallback was not needed.
- **Time-sensitive promotional pricing active on the access date itself:** SciSpace's "Flash sale till 22 September 2026" expires the same day this research was conducted; Paperpal's "Back To School Sale... up to 40% OFF" and Adobe's Creative Cloud "first year" student intro price are both open-ended but explicitly promotional. All annual/discounted figures for these three products should be treated as a snapshot of a promotional price, not necessarily each vendor's standing rate.
